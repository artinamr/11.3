Lesson 1 - Prebiotic Earth and Oparin-Haldane

Prebiotic Earth

Prebiotic Earth means the early Earth before any life.

There was no free oxygen in the air (reducing atmosphere). Instead there was water vapour, methane, ammonia and hydrogen.

There was also a lot of energy available. There were volcanoes, lightning, and UV from the sun because there was no ozone layer yet. Reactions need energy so this bit matters.

Oparin-Haldane

Oparin and Haldane said life didn't just appear one day. It built up gradually from non-living molecules.

The stages

1. Simple inorganic molecules were in the air and the ocean.

2. Energy made them react and form amino acids and simple sugars.

3. These built up in the ocean over millions of years. People call this the primordial soup.

4. The building blocks joined into bigger polymers, like proteins.

5. The polymers formed droplets with a boundary, and these became the first cells.
